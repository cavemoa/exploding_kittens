The core mechanic of **Exploding Kittens** is simple: play cards to avoid drawing an Exploding Kitten. If you draw one and can't defuse it, you explode and are out of the game.

Here is the breakdown of exactly what every card in the base deck does, followed by the rules for "Special Combos."

## The Life and Death Cards

* **Exploding Kitten:** If you draw this card, you must show it immediately. Unless you have a Defuse card, you explode, die, and are out of the game.
* **Defuse:** This is the most powerful card in the game. If you draw an Exploding Kitten, play this card immediately to save yourself. You then take the Exploding Kitten you just drew and secretly place it back into the draw pile *anywhere you want* (even right on top to blow up the next player).

---

## Action Cards

These cards are played face-up into the discard pile. You can play as many cards as you want during your turn, or none at all, but your turn always ends by drawing a card (unless you play an Attack or Skip).

* **Nope:** This stops any action *except* an Exploding Kitten or a Defuse card. Imagine the card beneath it never existed. You can play a Nope card at any time, even if it's not your turn. You can also play a Nope on top of another Nope to negate it (creating a "Yup"), and so on.
* **Attack:** Immediately end your turn without drawing a card, and force the next player to take two turns in a row. If the victim of an Attack plays an Attack card on their first turn, their turns are over, and the *next* player must take any remaining turns plus two more.
* **Skip:** Immediately end your turn without drawing a card. If you play a Skip card as a defense against an Attack, it only ends *one* of the two turns you are forced to take. You would need two Skip cards to end both turns.
* **Favor:** Force any other player to give you one card from their hand. They get to choose which card they give you.
* **Shuffle:** Randomly shuffle the draw pile without looking at the cards. This is a lifesaver if you know an Exploding Kitten is coming up.
* **See the Future:** Privately peek at the top three cards of the draw pile. Put them back in the exact same order without showing anyone else.

## Cat Cards & Special Combos

The deck includes several standard "Cat Cards" (like Taco Cat, Hairy Potato Cat, Rainbow-Ralphing Cat). On their own, these cards are completely powerless and do nothing. However, they become powerful when played in multiples, known as **Special Combos**.

*(Note: The game manual notes that these combo rules actually apply to ANY cards with the same title, not just the powerless Cat Cards!)*

* **Two of a Kind:** If you play any two cards with the exact same title (e.g., two Taco Cats, or two Shuffles), you get to blindly steal one random card from the hand of any other player.
* **Three of a Kind:** If you play any three cards with the exact same title, you get to name a specific card (like "Defuse") and demand it from another player. If they have it, they must give it to you. If they don't, you get nothing.
* **Five Different Cards:** If you play five cards that all have different titles, you get to look through the discard pile and take any single card you want. (If you take a Defuse, you must do it quickly before someone Nopes your combo!)

When you play cards as a combo, you ignore the printed instructions on the cards. For example, if you play two "See the Future" cards as a pair, you do not look at the top three cards; you only steal a random card from an opponent.
